## ✨ What it does

| Before your call | With Poof |
| --- | --- |
| Desktop full of files and screenshots | Just your wallpaper |
| Dock always showing | Dock tucked away |

- **One click.** Lives in your menu bar. No windows, no Dock icon, no settings.
- **Puts things back exactly.** If you already had the Dock on auto-hide, Poof leaves it that way.
- **Safe.** Quitting Poof, or even a crash, restores your desktop the next time it opens.

## 🚀 How to use it

1. Click the 👁 eye icon in the menu bar (top right of your screen).
2. Choose **Hide Everything**.
3. When you're done, choose **Show Everything**.

> [!NOTE]
> Open Finder windows close for a moment when icons are hidden or shown. That's normal.

## 📦 Download & install

1. Download **Poof.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/ahmadodeh9000/Poof/releases/latest).
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Poof.app** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open Poof. The first time, macOS will block it with a warning.

   This is expected: Poof isn't notarized by Apple, which is common for small free apps. To allow it:
   - Click **Done** on the warning.
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
   - Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Poof, then confirm.

   You only need to do this once.
4. Look for the eye icon in your menu bar. That's where Poof lives.

**Requirements:** macOS 26.5 or later.

## 🛠 Build from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ahmadodeh9000/Poof.git
open Poof/Poof.xcodeproj
```

Then press **Run** (⌘R) in Xcode.

## 🗑 Uninstall

Click the eye icon → **Quit**, then drag Poof from Applications to the Trash.
